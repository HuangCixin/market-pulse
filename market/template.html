    Market float

    Each sphere is a market. Height = how strongly it moved, in σ (its move divided by its usual move, so gold, oil, FX and crypto are comparable). Above the surface = up, below = down. Markets that usually move together sit close to each other; lines join strongly linked pairs. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover for details.

    Where the money moved

    Same date and period as the float. Bars show each market's move in σ; the number is the actual move (% or bp for yields). Markets are sorted strongest to weakest inside each group.
