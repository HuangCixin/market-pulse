    Market terrain

    A terrain built from every market's move. Height and color = how strongly it moved in σ (its move divided by its usual move, so gold, FX and crypto are comparable): peaks went up, valleys went down. Markets that usually move together sit close to each other, so a whole region rising or sinking shows money moving between groups. Dots mark each market; labels show σ and the actual % move. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover a dot for details.

    Where the money moved

    Same date and period as the terrain. Bars show each market's move in σ; the number is the actual move (% or bp for yields). Markets are sorted strongest to weakest inside each group.
